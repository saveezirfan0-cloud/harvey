# Product Knowledge: Skofi

Trained from skofi.tech's own content (services, case studies, regions and
profile copy). Every claim below is one the website makes in public. If it is
not here, Harvey does not say it.

## What We Sell
Skofi is an independent studio that builds the operational layer of a business:
the automations, integrations and applications that turn a set of tools into
something a team can run on. It works on Make.com, monday.com, Airtable,
HubSpot, Zapier and n8n, and in code where those run out.

## Core Value Proposition
Take the manual steps out of the work that actually runs the company, using the
tools the team already pays for, and hand over something the client owns
outright: the repository, the infrastructure accounts and the domain are the
client's from the first commit.

## Services
- **Business process automation**: take the manual steps out of the work that actually runs the company.
- **AI engineering and integration**: put models where they do measurable work, and keep a human on the decisions that matter.
- **Custom software development**: when the SaaS runs out, build the thing that fits the business.
- **Systems integration and APIs**: make the systems you already pay for behave as one.
- **Workflow automation**: Make.com, Zapier and n8n scenarios built to survive contact with production.
- **Ops platforms and CRM**: monday.com, Airtable and HubSpot set up so the team actually uses them.
- **Claude Code development and team setup**: software built with Claude Code, with an engineer reading every change.
- **Vibe-coded app rescue**: you prototyped it with AI; we make it safe to put real customers on.
- **MCP server development**: give AI agents controlled access to your systems, with permissions and a log.

timeline in writing.

## Target Audience
Operations teams at 10-200 person companies in the UK, US and Australia whose
quotes, orders, invoices or jobs move between several tools by hand.

## Two buyer segments: lead with the services that fit
| Segment | Buyers | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Operations-heavy businesses (installers, trades, logistics, staffing, distribution, agencies) | Operations leads, COOs, MDs, founders | Process automation, workflow automation, ops platforms and CRM, systems integration |
| Software and AI startups | Founders, CEOs, CTOs, engineering leads | AI integration, Claude Code development, vibe-coded app rescue, MCP servers, custom software |

Never send an ops-automation pitch to a software startup, or an engineering pitch to a trades business.

## Ideal Buyer Persona
Operations Manager, Head of Operations, COO, RevOps, or the founder/MD at a
company small enough that ops is still someone's evenings.

## Buying signals
- Runs on Make.com, Zapier, n8n, monday.com, Airtable or HubSpot (forms and embeds show on the site)
- Hiring an operations, automation, CRM admin or RevOps role
- Multi-site or field teams: installers, trades, logistics, staffing
- Quotes or job sheets as PDFs and spreadsheets
